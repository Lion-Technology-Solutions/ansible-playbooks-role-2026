How it works

  1. Create an account and receive a student number.
  2. Verify the registered email to activate the account.
  3. Set up an authenticator app, passkey or SMS code. After the grace period, setup is required before entering protected portal pages.
  4. After setup, verify a registered method on each sign-in.

Reset requests require the student number and matching registered email. Reset links expire after 30 minutes and can be used once. A password reset keeps two-factor protection and linked social identities enabled and invalidates existing sessions.

Authenticator apps and passkeys work without an SMS provider. SMS status: {{ 'Service configured' if sms_ready else 'Twilio Verify setup required' }}.

Existing students retain their student numbers, enrollments and payment status. Their security activation clock starts when their security profile activates under this system. Existing accounts get the configured grace period to verify their email and enroll a factor; new accounts must verify before activation. Administrators keep their existing separate sign-in.
