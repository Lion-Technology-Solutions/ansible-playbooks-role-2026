Connect a private management GPT

  1. Open the GPT editor. Keep the GPT private.
  2. Add an Action and import this schema: {{ schema_url }}.
  3. Choose API Key authentication, Bearer, and enter your portal connection key.
  4. Use our Privacy Policy for the Action privacy link.
  5. Test getPortalContext, then ask the GPT to prepare a small draft. Confirm only after reviewing its exact changes.

This does not automatically install tools into an existing chat. Use the connected GPT for future management conversations; availability in other chats depends on your ChatGPT account’s GPT/app features.
