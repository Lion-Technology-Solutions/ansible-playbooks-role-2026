Enrollment & payments

Which checkout should I use?

LionTech assigns Flutterwave for African residences and Stripe elsewhere. Confirm your country and review the exact local amount on your payment page. If checkout is unavailable, contact support.

{{ 'Open my programs' if g.student else 'Explore programs' }} →
I paid, but my payment is still pending.

Keep the provider reference and check payment activity in your portal. Contact support with your student number and reference before paying again. A checkout return screen by itself does not confirm enrollment.

Why can I see lessons but not class schedules?

Lesson outlines remain visible. Schedules and learning access require a qualifying verified payment, an approved enrollment and an active account.

Classes & resources

Where do I find my next class?

Open Class calendar in your student portal. Published sessions appear in your selected local time zone. Your Join button opens during the joining window.

Open class calendar →
Where are lesson resources and recordings?

Open your enrolled program and select the lesson. Your instructor publishes lesson resources, assignments, quizzes and available recordings there. Contact support if a resource you expect is missing.

Staff workspace

How do I activate a staff account?

Staff accounts start with an internal invitation. Accept the invitation using the invited work email and complete verification. Your administrator assigns your role and permissions.

Where do I find my tasks and daily reports?

Sign in to the staff workspace and open My tasks or Daily reports. Your submitted report stays in the portal; your supervisor receives the configured notification.
