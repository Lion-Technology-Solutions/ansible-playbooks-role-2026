# Admin-assisted enrollment and payment reconciliation

Student directory rows without a program have **Assign program**. Student details offer **Review enrollment & tuition**, with a popup and a complete-page fallback. **Assign another program** creates a separate enrollment rather than moving existing payments to another course.

Every manual approval confirms program/cohort, new/existing student status, agreed tuition and currency, an audit reason and the administrator's current password. Existing students can be assigned a historical cohort. Approval and access are separate: approving an unpaid request can leave access pending, while explicit verified-record attestation plus Activate program access authorizes eligibility. Declining requires a reason and blocks that program. Provider payment cannot bypass a pending or declined admin review. Standard self-service provider verification continues for enrollments without an admin review.

The individual-agreement mode records total tuition and total verified paid-to-date, including portal payments. Opening credit equals that paid-to-date figure minus existing ledger payments; cash entries remain intact. This credit counts toward the saved plan and student balance, but is not included as newly collected revenue. Monetary/currency mismatches, overpayments, stale forms and pending checkouts require reconciliation. Explicit confirmation is required to replace an existing individual agreement; prior terms, promotion details and the before/after snapshot are retained in the immutable reconciliation audit. Promotion records remain preserved and an explicit individual agreement takes precedence. Zero-tuition waivers require an extra confirmation.

The remaining balance is split into monthly, weekly or fortnightly installments from the agreed next due date. The last payment is capped at the exact remainder. The saved schedule is used by checkout, balance display, tuition status checks and reminders. Dates are interpreted in America/Toronto; notices use the student's local display currency and time zone.

Payment reminders run three days before, on the due date, three days overdue, then at most weekly between due dates. Existing daily maintenance is reused. Email is the primary channel. Students opt into WhatsApp and SMS at `/account/payment-alerts`; they can disable either channel. WhatsApp needs `TWILIO_PAYMENT_TEMPLATE_SID` (variables: student number, program, localized summary, portal payment URL). SMS uses `TWILIO_PAYMENT_SMS_FROM` with the existing configured Twilio account. Missing configuration stays visibly queued rather than claiming delivery. Every channel rechecks the outstanding amount before sending; settled or blocked notices cancel.

# Thirty-day recoverable deletion

The authenticated daily job evaluates actual unresolved invoices or installment arrears. It creates an individual 30-day warning window on first detection, including a fresh window for debts predating deployment. An account with no program or no quantifiable obligation is not automatically assumed to owe tuition. English/French daily email warnings use the student’s time zone and are targeted to eligible students only and show the due amount, remaining balance, deadline and support/payment links. A signed-in student also sees their own warning banner.

Whole-account deletion is held when another enrollment is paid/current, a staff/admin identity is linked, a manual suspension/access hold exists, a payment awaits verification, or an agreement is awaiting admin review. Clearing the amount actually due resolves the case even when later installments remain. Partial payments leaving the same arrears do not continually reset the timer. Settling earlier arrears and reaching a different due installment starts a new window.

At day 30, the transaction locks the student/enrollments and rechecks finances. At least one warning must have been accepted by the email provider, with at least seven days since that acceptance. Failed delivery extends the delivery grace period; it never silently causes deletion. If still eligible, the job creates the existing StudentArchive record, disables sign-in and invalidates student sessions. It does not physically delete student, payment, receipt, submission or audit rows. Repeated runs are idempotent. Restoration follows existing administrator controls and starts a fresh warning window if debt remains.

Administrators can review cases, extend an individual deadline by 30 days, and pause/resume the policy at `/admin/payment-deletion`. Policy changes require reauthentication and are audited. Resuming starts fresh warning windows for held cases. `PAYMENT_RETENTION_ENABLED=False` is also available as an application-config operational switch; confirmed payment schedules are retained.

All new tables are additive through the portal's existing metadata initialization. Deployment does not immediately archive old accounts or send a bulk warning campaign: cases are evaluated by the next authenticated daily run. Do not hard-delete financial or audit records during rollback.
