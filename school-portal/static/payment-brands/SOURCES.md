# Payment brand assets

Brand marks identify payment options; their presence does not imply provider availability or endorsement.

- Visa and Stripe: Simple Icons v14, https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons (CC0; marks remain their owners’ trademarks).
- Flutterwave: official primary full-colour mark, https://flutterwave.com/us/press-kit
- MTN: official corporate mark, https://www.mtn.com/wp-content/themes/mtn-refresh/public/img/mtn-logo.svg
- Orange: official corporate mark, https://www.orange.cm/particuliers/resources/img/master-logo.svg
- Interac: https://github.com/rafara/paymentslogos/blob/master/interac.svg ; mark belongs to Interac Corp.
- Generic mobile, bank, cash and transfer symbols: Lucide v0.468.0, https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide (ISC).

Only SVG root dimensions were made explicit for consistent standalone rendering. Provider marks were not redrawn.
